Jarvis

A butler that lives on my Mac. It listens, decides who should do each job, and uses the Mac for me.

See its hands work ↓

The whole tour in 82 seconds.

Start here: the voice, the HUD, the buttons and the hands, in one short film. It has sound.

Hold ⌘M and talk.

Hold ⌘M like a walkie-talkie, or double-tap it for hands-free. Whisper turns my voice into text locally, on the Mac.

Notch island
Hover the notch to see what is next, the last answer and a music card.
Inside Obsidian
The HUD also runs as an Obsidian plugin, and every order leaves a ticket in the vault.
The mirror
Reads 269 of my chats with Claude, finds what I correct most and proposes CLAUDE.md changes. Nothing applies until I approve.

Then it decides who does the job.

Plain rules check every order first: no model, 0 ms. If they are unsure, JEV picks a route in about 0.4 s and the job goes to Gemini or Claude.

It answers out loud.

Replies come back in a British butler voice. One click plans the day: classes, deadlines, events and three priorities, written into today's Obsidian note.

Also one click: close the day, score the week from 1 to 10, sort the inbox into urgent, reply and ignore with drafts (it never sends), find internships that fit, and check my CV against a posting.

Then its hands use my Mac.

Say "open Spotify" and a pointer flies to the Dock and clicks. Jarvis reads any window through macOS Accessibility, so it is not limited to its own app.

Scroll, volume, media keys
Play, pause, next and volume by voice. Irreversible actions always ask first.
