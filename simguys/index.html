Sim Guys

A free iPhone fight game. Pick a fighter, aim the first hit, watch the brawl.

What it is

A fan-made tribute to the YouTube creator SpennTF2 and his Guy vs Guy fights. Not affiliated with or endorsed by him.

Help

Something not working, or an idea? Write to nsanbenitop@gmail.com. I answer within a few days.

Purchases. Gems and the Starter Pack are bought through Apple. If something you paid for is missing, open the shop and tap Restore purchases. For refunds, use reportaproblem.apple.com.

Delete your data. There is no account. Your progress lives on your iPhone and your iCloud. To remove your ghost or your weekly cup entry, email me and I delete it.
