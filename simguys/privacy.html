Sim Guys: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026 · Español

Sim Guys is a free game made by Ignacio San Benito (Madrid, Spain). This page says what the app stores and who gets it.

No account

The game has no sign-up and no login of its own. We never see your email, your real name or your location.

What stays on your iPhone and iCloud

Your coins, gems, collection and progress are saved on your device and copied to your own iCloud. We cannot read them.

Your “ghost” (Apple CloudKit)

After a fight against a player, the app uploads your fighters, your rank points and the direction you aimed, plus a random ID created by the game, to a public Apple CloudKit database, so other players can fight your ghost. It contains no name or text. It is replaced after each fight.

Weekly cup

When you finish a weekly cup match, the app sends our server (hosted on Cloudflare) your Game Center nickname (or “Participant” if you are not signed in to Game Center), a random ID created by the game, and the fights you played. The server keeps your points, wins and matches for that week’s table, which every player can see. Only a scrambled version of the random ID is shown. Each week starts a new table.

Game Center

Your rank points, wins and achievements go to Apple’s Game Center under your Game Center player. Apple’s privacy policy applies to that.

Purchases

Payments are handled by Apple. We only learn, on your device, which items you have bought, to give them to you.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The game shows optional rewarded video ads from Google. Google may use your device’s advertising identifier and ad data to show and measure ads. In the EU and UK you are asked for consent first. iOS also asks if the app may track you; if you say no, you still get ads, but not personalised. You can change this in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. See policies.google.com/privacy.

Children

The game is not aimed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Your rights (GDPR)

To get or delete your ghost or your weekly cup entries, email us and we do it. To delete Game Center data, use Apple’s tools.
